2. Gamification: Turning Stress into a "Level Up"
To drive daily engagement—crucial for "non-digital" users—we implement a Duolingo-style financial journey:

The Freedom Score: A single, dynamic number (0-100) that moves in real-time as users adjust their "Sensitivity Sliders" (Return Rate, Savings Rate).

Interactive "What-Ifs": A single slider allows users to see the sensitivity of their return—showing how an 8% vs 11% return changes their freedom date by years—without needing to understand math.
