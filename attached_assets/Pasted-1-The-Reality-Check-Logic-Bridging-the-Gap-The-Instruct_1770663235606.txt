1. The "Reality Check" Logic (Bridging the Gap)
The Instruction: "If a Gap exists (Required Capital > Current Projection), the UI must dynamically display a 'Solution Module' with three toggleable paths. This removes the 'deception' of showing a huge gap without a way out."

Option A: The Savings Lever.

Prompt: "To close this gap, you need to increase your monthly savings by [Dynamic Amount]."

Option B: The Lump Sum 'Catch-up'.

Prompt: "A one-time injection of [Dynamic Amount] today would put you back on track for your target age."

Option C: The Efficiency Lever (The Smart Manager).

Prompt: "By optimizing your strategy for an extra [X]% return, you close the gap without saving another cent. (Reference the Sensitivity Analysis here)."

2. The "Surplus" Logic (The Positive Hook)
The Instruction: "If the user has a Surplus (Current Projection > Required Capital), change the tone to 'Optimization' rather than 'Rescue'."

The Reward: "You have a surplus of [Amount]. This means you could potentially retire [X] years earlier, at age [New Age]."

The Upsell: "Or, you could maintain your retirement age but increase your monthly spending budget to [New Monthly Amount]."

3. Transitioning to the Risk Assessment (The "How")
The Instruction: "Directly link the 'Efficiency Lever' to the Risk Assessment. If the user wants to see how to get that extra 2% return, they must 'Unlock their Investor DNA'."
